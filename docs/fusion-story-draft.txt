Title:  Hult Prize UWindsor has a home
Date:  September 29, 2026     Category:  Story
Excerpt:  Fusion opened on the second floor of the Joyce Entrepreneurship Centre, and we have an HQ inside it.

Fusion launched on September 29 on the second floor of the Joyce Entrepreneurship Centre, and Hult Prize UWindsor has an HQ inside it.

Fusion is a hub for people building things. Somewhere to work, somewhere to run into someone building their own thing, somewhere events actually happen. Our teams can work and brainstorm there, meet people outside their own program, and we will be running sessions and events out of it all year.

For a program that spent its first year in borrowed rooms, that changes what we can do. A workshop does not have to be booked six weeks ahead. A team that wants to argue about its business model at four on a Tuesday afternoon has somewhere to do it. And students who have never heard of the Hult Prize will walk past us on their way somewhere else, which is how most people find this competition in the first place.

Thank you to [NAME] and the team at Fusion for backing this before we had anything to show for the year.

It is our home, but it is not a closed door. If you are a UWindsor student with an idea, or half an idea, come and find us.
