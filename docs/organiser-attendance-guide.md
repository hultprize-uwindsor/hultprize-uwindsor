# Organiser attendance guide

1. Open `/organiser/events` and sign in using the organiser password supplied through the team's secure channel. Use the support organiser account only when dietary/accessibility information is needed. Never share an attendee's private management link.
2. Choose one or more events, then filter by category, institution, faculty, team, registration status or name/email. **Unique people**, **event registrations**, **confirmed attendance** and **actual check-ins** measure different things. One person attending five workshops is one person and five registrations. Student categories come from what the attendee selected.
3. Use the CSV export for the current event/filter selection. These are operational rosters; they exclude dietary and accessibility information. Do not send contact lists to sponsors or partners. Store exports in restricted team storage and delete them under the retention schedule.
4. At the door, find the person and check in their registration for that event. Register walk-ins individually with the same essential fields and acknowledgement. Never count a team name as several attendees, and never check someone in for all events when they attended only one. Check-ins also decide the follow-up emails: everyone checked in gets a thank-you card that evening, and confirmed registrants who weren't checked in get a “We missed you” letter at 10 AM the next morning. So check everyone in, including late arrivals, and fix any missed check-in the same evening.
5. In event settings, edit the title, description, local Windsor date/time, full venue, preparation notes, status, capacity and late-registration setting. Leave capacity blank until a venue limit is confirmed. Registration normally closes when the event begins.
6. After a material date, time or venue change, use **Send event update** for that event. Review the affected event carefully before sending. Changing the schedule and sending the message are separate actions so you can review the final details.
7. A full event offers waitlisting alongside other confirmable events. When a confirmed attendee cancels, the next waitlisted person receives an expiring seat offer. Their status stays offered until they accept; they are not silently confirmed. Reserved offers count against capacity. The scheduled maintenance job expires unaccepted offers and advances the queue.
8. Check email delivery failures and retry after fixing a quota or configuration problem. A failed email does not delete or duplicate the saved registration. Reminder messages are scheduled approximately 24 hours before an event and exclude cancelled events/registrations.
9. Attendees can request a fresh management link at `/attend/manage`, add another event, cancel one event and update dietary/accessibility needs. Repeat registration submissions require verification before existing records can be changed.
10. For corrections, deletion requests or optional-update withdrawal, use the contact address on `/privacy` and verify the requester's identity. Follow the documented retention process and remove downloaded copies as well as application records. Log out when finished, especially on a shared check-in device.

Before each event, confirm the live schedule and capacity, inspect confirmed and waitlisted totals, prepare support requirements using the restricted view, and check for failed messages. Report registration totals separately from actual check-ins afterward.

After each event, thank-you cards go out automatically from 7 PM Windsor time and missed-you letters from 10 AM the next morning (see [email templates](event-email-templates.md#thank-you-cards-and-missed-you-letters)). If someone replies that they were there, check them in for that event. If someone asks to stop hearing about other events, tell the site maintainer: there is no dashboard control for that yet. Check the Email delivery tab for failed `thanks` and `missed` messages. If a send was missed, the site maintainer can run `sendFollowUpsForLatestEvent()` in Apps Script.
